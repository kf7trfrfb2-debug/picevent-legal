3. How albums work

  • Each album can only be accessed with its invitation code. You are responsible for sharing that code.
  • Videos last 3 minutes at most. Without an Event Pass, only their first 30 seconds can be played; the full video is kept and becomes playable if the organiser buys the Pass, including videos already sent.

  • Each album expires automatically 5 months after its creation: all its content is then permanently deleted. Remember to save your photos before it expires.
