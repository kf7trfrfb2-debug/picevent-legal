This page lists what the PicEvent app distributes without having created it: the images on its welcome screen and the typeface of its logo. It has nothing to do with the photos you put in your own albums — those are yours, and what happens to them is described in the privacy policy.

The logo typeface

The P of PicEvent is set in Butler ExtraBold, a typeface created by Fabian De Smet and distributed for free at www.fabiandesmet.com.

Its licence allows commercial use in so many words: “The Typeface is free to use for personal and commercial creative work.” The file is embedded in the app as is, with no modification whatsoever, and the full licence travels with it.

Butler remains the property of its author. The licence grants the right to use the typeface, not ownership of it.

Wrongly credited image?

If you are the author of an image used here and the credit looks wrong or incomplete, write to piceventcontacte@gmail.com and we will correct it or take the image down.
